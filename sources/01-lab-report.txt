INTERNAL LAB REPORT — Perovskite Tandem Cell Line B
Date: 11 March 2025
Author: Materials Characterisation Group

Cell B-14, a silicon/perovskite tandem, reached a certified power conversion
efficiency of 27.4 percent under standard AM1.5G illumination. This is the
highest figure recorded on Line B to date. The previous best on the same line
was 25.9 percent, measured in November 2024.

The active perovskite layer used a formamidinium-caesium mixed cation
composition deposited by two-step vapour-assisted coating. Average layer
thickness across the batch was 640 nanometres.

Stability remains the limiting factor. Under continuous one-sun illumination at
65 degrees Celsius, cell B-14 retained 82 percent of its initial efficiency
after 1,000 hours. Three sibling cells from the same batch retained between 74
and 80 percent over the same period. We did not test beyond 1,000 hours.

Encapsulation was a single-layer glass-glass stack with a butyl rubber edge
seal. Cells without encapsulation degraded below 50 percent of initial
efficiency within 200 hours in the same conditions.

We make no claim about outdoor performance. All measurements in this report were
taken indoors under controlled conditions.
