TRADE PRESS BRIEF — "Perovskites edge closer to the field"
Solar Manufacturing Weekly, Issue 214, April 2025

Several manufacturers have moved perovskite-silicon tandem modules from the lab
bench to pilot production lines this year. Analysts at Grieve & Co. estimate
that pilot-scale annual capacity across all announced European lines totals
roughly 300 megawatts, a small fraction of the multi-gigawatt silicon industry.

Costs remain uncertain. Grieve & Co. put current pilot-line module cost at
between 0.34 and 0.52 dollars per watt, compared with about 0.11 dollars per
watt for mature silicon modules. The analysts stress that these pilot figures
are not representative of mass production and decline to forecast a crossover
date.

Two obstacles are repeatedly cited by manufacturers: long-term stability under
heat and humidity, and the lack of an accepted accelerated-ageing standard for
perovskite devices. The IEC working group on the subject has not published a
final test protocol.

No commercial perovskite tandem module currently carries a 25-year performance
warranty. The longest warranty offered by any of the manufacturers contacted for
this brief was 10 years.
