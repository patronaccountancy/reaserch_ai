FIELD TRIAL NOTE — Rooftop Array, Site 7 (Coastal, Humid Subtropical)
Reporting period: June 2024 to May 2025

Site 7 hosts 48 perovskite-silicon tandem modules alongside a reference string
of 48 conventional silicon modules. Both strings share the same inverter model,
tilt and azimuth.

Over the twelve-month reporting period the tandem string produced 9 percent more
energy per installed watt-peak than the silicon reference string. The advantage
was largest in the cooler months and narrowed to near zero during the humid
July-August period.

Four tandem modules were removed from service during the period. Three showed
visible delamination at the module edge; one failed after a junction-box water
ingress event that also affected two silicon modules. No silicon modules were
removed for delamination.

Measured degradation across the surviving tandem modules averaged 3.1 percent
over the twelve months, against 0.6 percent for the silicon reference. Twelve
months is too short a window to extrapolate a lifetime degradation rate, and we
do not do so here.

This is a single site. Results should not be generalised to other climates.
